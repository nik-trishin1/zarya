## Why "aim narrower", not a clean yes
A business idea is tested by walking a chain: is there money in the task → is there a buyable customer → is there value they notice → do the economics work → can you reach them.

- **Money in the gathering itself:** yes (food, going out). You are not that cash register.
- **Money in the invite / RSVP tool:** historically no. [Partiful](https://www.partiful.com/) still charges $0. [Timepad](https://secretmag.ru/business/trade-secret/vy-zaregistrirovany-kak-servis-timepad-prodayot-bilety-na-1-mlrd-rublej-v-god.htm) tried a host subscription years ago (2 → 7 paying accounts) and lives on ticket commission instead. Free registrations on Timepad were **5×** paid tickets in 2025 ([RBC Life](https://rbclifemedia.ru/news/69ef331d9a7947165804daa7)).
- **Buyable customer for the current app:** unproven. The circle has not been invited. The PRD cut the world to “20 friends” before mapping who already pays for this task.
- **Value vs the current way:** the current way is the messenger chat + poll. That is free, already open, and good enough for many hosts. The Mini App only wins if the chat is already failing (mixed circles, lost headcount, nagging).
- **Economics:** 0 ₽ by design.
- **Reach:** Telegram Mini Apps are native **if** the circle stays on Telegram. [Mediascope via Techora](https://techora.ru/news/max-obognal-telegram-po-mesyachnoy-auditorii-2026-08-02) (June 2026): Max 86.2M monthly / 69M daily vs Telegram 75.7M / 35.4M in Russia 12+.

So: **not a clean yes** (that would mean “go build more”). **Not a pivot-away-from-the-house yet** (the 20-person run is still the cheapest test of the current shape). **Aim narrower:** treat what you built as a probe of one host’s Telegram circle; stop the feature factory; if you later want a company, change the task you compete for. ### S1 — Unpaid host of an existing Telegram circle (no-nag first) ✅

> **I propose we focus on this segment because** it is the only group the current Mini App can fully serve without changing the Core Job, and it is the cheapest probe. The selection screen does **not** compose into a company: value maybe, margin weak, demand weak, size weak. Focus = validate, not scale.
> **Coverage:** a small slice of gathering-coordination; not ~80% of *money*.

#### Why this segment is attractive
Urgency is social and emotional (leftover food, empty chairs, being the nag), not a budget line. Reachability is perfect if you already have the circle. Attractiveness as a *business* is low; attractiveness as a *probe of the house thesis* is high.

#### Persona
The person who already threw the last three dinners or weekends, already has everyone in one Telegram chat, and already ran the “кто идёт?” loop last month. They are not “25–40.” They are defined by **having hosted** and **already living in that chat**.
- **Already hosts repeatedly** — cause: past performance is the only honest sample; fantasy “I should host more” is a Fake Job.
- **Circle already lives in one messenger thread** — cause: switching cost out of the chat is huge; you only win if you sit *on* that channel.
- **Chasing RSVPs is a Tax Job they already perform** — cause: that is the under-delivery of the poll; without it they will not move.
- **No-stress / don’t-become-the-nag ranks first** — cause: inverted priority vs “I want to see who else is coming” vs “I need paid seats covered” = different segments.

#### Core Jobs
1. **When** I have already named a date for my circle in our Telegram chat, I feel irritated at pinging silent people, **I want to** get a yes/no (and extras) without chasing each person **with success criteria** headcount known ~24h out; I am not the nag; nobody needed a new login, **in order to** gather the circle in person and feel the night is real, not a maybe.
2. **When** the night is on, **I want to** have it on my own calendar **with success criteria** one tap, correct time/place, **in order to** show up without forgetting.

#### Big Jobs (motivation context above the Core Jobs)
*Personal:* **I want to** keep this circle actually meeting **in order to** not let the friendships live only as a chat.

### Positioning (the Core Job → Big Job link — headline first)

**Mechanic direction:** kill a Job as a class — kill *chase each person for a yes* — and remove the host’s nagging/anxiety emotion. ### Threat from Big-Job-level players
A friend who just hosts, and Telegram itself, close the Big Job without software. That threat is **high** for S1. Invites/Timepad are a threat only if you pivot to S2.

### Detailed validation plan — per assumption, step by step

**Assumption 1: Past hosts in Telegram circles actually chase RSVPs**
- **Method:** AJTBD customer interviews on **past behavior** (not “would you use a Mini App?”). Recruit people who hosted in the last 90 days.
- **Steps:** 1) List 10 people who hosted (founder’s circle + adjacent). 2) 45-min call. 3) Ask: last gathering — situation, trigger, what they did first, what they used, how they knew who was coming, what they judged as “good enough,” what they did the morning of. - **Cost / time:** founder calendar; no new code.

**Assumption 2: They will switch off the poll**
- **Method:** concierge / live probe (the Mini App already exists — do not rebuild).
- **Steps:** Invite the 20 with one sentence: the next gathering lives in the bot. Host creates the event as usual. Do **not** also post a poll. After the event, screenshot the chat: did a parallel “я иду” thread appear anyway?
- **Kill criterion:** if after two gatherings the chat still carries the source of truth, the Mini App is extra surface — subtract features, do not add T-301/T-304.
- **Cost / time:** two gathering cycles (whatever the circle’s natural frequency is).

**Assumption 3: There is a Job budget that can fund a product**
- **Method:** in the same interviews, ask what they already spent (tools, leftover food, time). For a company thesis, add a priced conversation only with S2 operators (commission analog, not a fake landing).
- **Steps:** Record ₽ and hours. Compare to Railway + founder time.
- **Kill criterion (company):** if tool spend is 0 and frequency < ~1 gathering / month, do not raise a business on S1. House tool may continue.
- **Cost / time:** same interviews.

**Assumption 4: Telegram remains the circle’s home**
- **Method:** observation + public reach trend, not a survey about “favorite messenger.”
- **Steps:** Ask “where did this group actually write last week?” Watch whether people already use Max workarounds. Re-read Mediascope-class coverage quarterly ([June 2026 snapshot](https://techora.ru/news/max-obognal-telegram-po-mesyachnoy-auditorii-2026-08-02)).
