#### Persona
Someone who already billed members last month **and** held a live breakfast/workshop/club night. Not “a Telegram admin.” Causal:

- **Payers-in-the-room ranks before public social proof** — cause: inverted vs Luma/Invites guest lists.

#### Core Jobs
1. **When** members are paid-up and a live night is dated, **I want to** get payers into that gathering **with success criteria** only paid people on the list; reminders they actually read; cap if the room is scarce, **in order to** make the club feel real enough that they renew.
2. **When** someone stops paying, **I want to** drop them from events and the chat **with success criteria** one status, not two lists, **in order to** not host free-riders.

#### Big Jobs
*Business:* **I want to** keep monthly membership income **in order to** run the club without it dying into a free chat.
*Personal:* **I want to** stop being the evening admin **in order to** not dread the next meetup.
